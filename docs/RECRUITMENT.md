# Reviewer recruitment

Three send-ready messages, plus a log of what has actually gone out. Edit the bracketed
bits and send. **Log every send** at the bottom — the Dec 20 checkpoint (ROADMAP.md M9)
decides whether Q1 continues as planned or pivots to direct outreach, and that decision
needs to know what was actually tried.

**The ask is deliberately small.** One chapter. Two hours. The instinct is to ask for a
committed reviewer; the thing people say yes to is one chapter they already teach.

---

## 1. Email / listserv

> **Subject: Would you read one chapter of a free middle school US history textbook?**
>
> Hi [NAME],
>
> I teach 8th grade US history, and over the past year I've been building a free,
> openly licensed textbook for grades 6–8 — an adaptation of *The American Yawp*, the
> collaboratively written college text, rewritten for 11–14 year olds. It's online, it's
> CC BY-SA, and it will never be behind a paywall or on Teachers Pay Teachers.
>
> It's also honest about what it is: the chapters were drafted with AI assistance and
> haven't been vetted by enough humans. That's the problem I'm writing to you about.
>
> **No chapter is marked ready for classrooms until three educators have reviewed it.
> Right now every chapter sits at zero.** I'd rather the site say that plainly than
> pretend otherwise.
>
> Would you read **one chapter** — ideally one you already teach — and tell me what's
> wrong with it? Roughly two hours. There's a form that asks what you'd expect: factual
> errors, whether the reading level is right, whether the perspectives are handled
> honestly.
>
> Start here: [https://americanyawpms.com/teachers.html]
>
> If it's not for you, no need to reply. If you know someone it might be for, forwarding
> this is the single most useful thing you could do.
>
> Thanks,
> [YOUR NAME]

## 3. GitHub Discussions / open-education forum

> **Looking for 3 reviewers per chapter — 45 reviews, currently at 0**
>
> American Yawp MS is a CC BY-SA 4.0 middle school adaptation of *The American Yawp*,
> covering Volume I (pre-Columbian through Reconstruction). Static HTML, no tracking, no
> paywall, deployed at americanyawpms.com.
>
> The content was drafted with AI assistance. The project's own bar is that **no chapter
> is "classroom ready" until three educators sign off**, and that status is published per
> chapter rather than buried. The Primary Source Reader has already been through a full
> audit — all 61 sources carry verified links to the original.
>
> **The ask:** claim one chapter's Review slot issue, read it, file a review. The
> template asks which version you read and whether we may credit you. How a review counts
> toward the bar is written down in REVIEW_STATUS.md — deliberately, before the first
> review arrived, so no rule gets invented to fit a verdict.
>
> Reviewers with SPED or multilingual-learner experience are especially wanted; the
> reading tools have not been tested with a screen reader yet, and that gap is on the
> roadmap rather than hidden.
>
> Repo: https://github.com/shiebenaderet/yawpms
